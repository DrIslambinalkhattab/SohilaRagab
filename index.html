    03 — Expertise

    Six areas, one focus: clear, confident communication.

    01

    German conversation & fluency training

    Regular, structured practice that helps learners use everyday German effectively in real-life situations.

    • Structured weekly speaking practice built around real-life scenarios
    • Live correction and feedback during conversation, not after it
    • Progressive difficulty matched to the learner’s CEFR level
    02

    Assessment & placement testing

    Language interviews and level frameworks that place learners — and candidates for trainer roles — accurately.

    • Structured language interviews mapped to CEFR level descriptors
    • Consistent scoring criteria applied across every candidate
    • Clear, actionable next step for each learner or trainer candidate
    03

    Speaking & pronunciation coaching

    Correcting common speaking errors and refining pronunciation while steadily building learner confidence.

    • Targeting the most frequent pronunciation and grammar errors
    • Focused drills for sounds, rhythm and intonation
    • Confidence-building techniques for hesitant speakers
    04

    Curriculum & conversation material development

    Designing conversation materials, curricula and level frameworks for multiple training systems.

    • Conversation materials aligned to each level and training goal
    • Curricula and level frameworks adaptable across training systems
    • Materials refined continuously from real learner feedback
